Family Books

Every Story Feels Like Home.

A heritage reading room for the digital age

Family Books was imagined as the kind of bookstore you remember from childhood visits with parents — warm wood, quiet corners, and covers that invite you to stay a little longer. This online system carries that atmosphere into a polished web experience while remaining academically rigorous.

Built for SE1020 Object Oriented Programming at SLIIT, the platform demonstrates encapsulation, inheritance, polymorphism, abstraction, and information hiding through a clean Spring Boot architecture with text-file persistence.

What you can do here

  • Browse a curated collection of printed books and e-books
  • Register securely and manage your profile
  • Leave thoughtful reviews on titles you love
  • Administer catalogue, categories, users, and reviews (admin)
